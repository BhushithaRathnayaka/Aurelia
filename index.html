Our Story

A Table Set in Shadow & Light

Aurelia began with a single charcoal grill and a stubborn belief that simplicity, done properly, is the highest form of luxury. Our dining room is wrapped in deep charcoal, warmed by cream linen and lit by the soft flicker of brass and gold.

Head Chef Nadeesha Fernando sources from small farms in Nuwara Eliya, coastal boats in Negombo, and a network of growers who share our obsession with flavour over spectacle.

  • Open Fire Kitchen

    Every protein passes over live coals.

  • Seasonal Only
